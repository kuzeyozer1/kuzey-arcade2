❔ HOW TO PLAY

Move with WASD / arrows, or put your finger anywhere and slide (a joystick appears). Your weapons attack by themselves.

Monsters drop 💎 gems. Collect them to level up and choose a new weapon or make one stronger. You can hold 5 weapons and 5 power items.

Big monsters drop 🎁 chests with a free upgrade. 🍗 heals you, 🧲 pulls in every gem.

Bosses come at 5:00 and 10:00. Survive to 10:00 and beat the last boss to win! Coins you collect buy heroes and power-ups.
