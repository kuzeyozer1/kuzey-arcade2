❔ HOW TO PLAY

Notes slide down four lanes. When a note touches the glowing line, hit its lane:

Keyboard: D F J K (or ← ↓ ↑ →). Touch: tap the lane at the bottom of the screen. Long notes: hold until the tail ends!

PERFECT, GREAT and GOOD keep your combo going. Missing drains the energy bar — if it runs out the song stops (not on Easy). Fill the ⚡ fever bar with combos for double points!

If the notes feel early or late on your device, change TIMING in the menu.
