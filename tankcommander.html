❔ HOW TO PLAY

Keyboard + mouse: WASD drives (W forward, A/D turn), the mouse aims your turret, click to fire. Q drops a mine, Shift boosts.

Touch: left thumb drives the tank the way you push, right thumb aims and fires.

🧱 Bricks break. ⬛ Steel bounces your shells — use it for trick shots! 🌊 Water stops tanks but not shells. 🌳 Bushes hide you from enemies.

Missions: destroy all enemy tanks, blow up the 📡 radio towers that call in help, or beat a boss tank. Pick up 🔧 repairs, 🛡️ shields and ⚡ power shots.
