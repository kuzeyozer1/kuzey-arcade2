HOW TO PLAY

Move: arrow keys or WASD, or drag anywhere on the screen with your finger or mouse. Your ship fires by itself.

Bomb: Space or B, or the 💣 button. A bomb clears every enemy bullet on screen and hurts all enemies.

Focus: hold Shift (or put two fingers down) to fly slowly and show your tiny hit box. Only the glowing dot in the middle of your ship can be hit!

Graze: fly very close to enemy bullets without touching them for bonus points.

Combo: keep shooting enemies quickly to raise your multiplier up to ×8. Getting hit resets it.

Pick-ups: P = more power, S = shield, B = bomb, + = repair, 🧲 = magnet, 🪙 = coins.

Pause: P or Esc.
