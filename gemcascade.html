❔ HOW TO PLAY

Swap two gems next to each other (drag, or tap one then the other) to line up 3 or more of the same colour.

Match 4 → a striped gem that blasts a whole row or column. Match in an L or T → a bomb gem that blows up around it. Match 5 → a 🌈 rainbow gem that removes every gem of one colour. Swap two special gems together for a mega combo!

❄️ Ice breaks when you make a match on it. Some levels want you to collect certain colours or break all the ice before you run out of moves.

Boosters: 🔨 hammer smashes one gem, 🔀 shuffle mixes the board, ➕5 gives 5 more moves.
