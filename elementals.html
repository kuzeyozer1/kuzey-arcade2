Elementals

Fire, Wind, Water, Lightning and Earth. Drink an element potion, forge its weapon, then fight your way through five temples and two new dimensions.

Get the modPick your element

Five elements

Drink a potion. Pick your weapon.

Drinking an element potion sets your element. A slot above the hotbar shows it, and it stays through death until you drink a different one. Every weapon has two abilities and a third that needs its potion.

Five temples

Every temple has a boss guarding its block.

Each boss spawns once, when a player comes near, with a boss bar. Follow the Temple Compass: it points to the nearest temple in your dimension whose boss is still alive.

Two new dimensions

Go above the clouds, or into the storm.

The Aether

Floating islands of Aether Grass, Aether Dirt and Holystone, with skyroot trees, Skyblooms and Sky Crystal.

  • Biomes: Floating Meadows, Sky Forest, Crystal Peaks
  • Mobs: Cloud Sheep, Sky Gliders, Storm Wisps
  • Home of the Wind Temple, on its own floating island

Portal: build a glowstone frame like a Nether portal, then use a water bucket on it.

The Lightning Dimension

A permanent thunderstorm over Storm Soil, Storm Stone, Storm Sand, Fulgurite Glass and Charged Stone spires. The lightning is only for looks.

  • Biomes: Storm Plains, Charged Spires, Glass Fields
  • Mobs: Sparks, Storm Golems
  • Home of the Lightning Temple

Portal: light a Nether portal frame with Flint and Copper, or let lightning strike its top obsidian.

Crafting

It all starts with Elemental Ore.

Elemental Ore shows on cave walls in the Overworld from y -64 to 16, most often around y -24, and is about as common as diamond. You also find it in the Aether and the Lightning Dimension.

Commands

Bind your abilities to keys.

Every new player gets an interactive Guide Book with buttons that run these. Ability commands work for everyone; the function commands are for operators.

Abilities 1 and 2 have a 20 second cooldown and potion abilities 40 seconds, starting when you use them. Your own lightning never hurts you.

Install

Ready in four steps.

Install Fabric

Install Fabric Loader 0.16 or newer for Minecraft 1.21.1.

Add Fabric API

Put the Fabric API jar in your mods folder.

Add Elementals

Put elementals-3.7.0.jar in the same mods folder.

Play

Start the Fabric profile. Your Guide Book is waiting on first join.
