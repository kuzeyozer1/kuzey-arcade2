📘 PIECE GUIDE

How to play: tap a piece on the board to turn it. Pieces with a 🔒 dot can't move. Pick a piece from the tray underneath and tap an empty square to place it. Tap a placed piece to turn it, or press and hold it (or right-click) to put it back. Light every target with the right colour!

Keyboard: arrow keys move the yellow cursor, Space or Enter turns or places a piece, Backspace takes it back, 1–9 pick from the tray, U = undo, R = restart, H = hint, Esc = back.

🛠️ LEVEL MAKER

Pick a piece and tap the board. Tap again to turn it. Use Movable to choose if players can turn a piece (🔄) or must place it from the tray (✋). Then press TEST — you must solve your own puzzle before you can share it.
