❓ HOW TO PLAY

Tap, click, Space or ↓ drops the sliding block. Anything hanging over the edge falls off, so the tower gets thinner.

Perfect drop: land it right on top. Three perfects in a row make the block grow back a little.

Power-ups come every 10 floors, or from 🎁 gift blocks: 🐢 Slow (key 1) slows the next 5 blocks, ↔️ Wide (key 2) makes your block full size again, 🛡️ Shield (key 3) saves you from one miss.

Gift blocks 🎁 sometimes slide in — land them to win a power-up or coins.

Climb past the clouds, the planes, the satellites… and see what is at the top. Your avatar rides the tower with you!

P or Esc pauses.
