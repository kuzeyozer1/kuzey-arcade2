KUZEY WORLD

A whole town to play in! Make characters, decorate rooms, find hidden gifts and make up your own stories.

👥 MY CHARACTERS

Tap someone to change how they look. In town you can also tap 👕 to dress the last person you touched.

✨ NEW CHARACTER

❔ HOW TO PLAY

🖐️ Drag people, food and toys anywhere. Put people on sofas, chairs and beds, on the swing or at the top of the slide!

🍰 Give someone food by dropping it on their hand. They eat it bite by bite. Drop a crown, hat or sunglasses on someone's head to put it on.

👆 Tap things to see what happens: the fridge, the café counter, the toy box, the TV, the lamp, the pet cages…

🎒 Use the backpack at the bottom to carry people and things to another place. Drop them in a slot, change place, then drag them out.

💇 Sit someone in a salon chair to change their hair. Tap the wardrobe at home or 👕 to change clothes.

🗺️ Tap Map to see the whole town. Tap a building to go inside. In the house you can visit every room.

🎁 Every place has a hidden gift. Watch for a tiny twinkle and tap it! Find 10 gifts to open the 🚀 Space Station.

🎨 Tap Decorate to change walls and floors, move furniture, and add pictures, plants, beds, lamps and more.

🩻 Stand someone in the X-ray at the hospital. Put someone in the bubble bath. Grow vegetables at the farm, build a sandcastle at the beach, and turn on the disco ball on the stage!

✨ Make babies, kids, teens and grown-ups. Give them wings, capes, gowns, suits and more. Tap someone to see how they feel.

🌙 Switch between day and night, change the weather outside, and tap 📸 to save a photo. Everything is saved by itself.

📱↻ Turn your phone sideways for a bigger town!
