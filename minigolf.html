HOW TO PLAY

Shoot: press anywhere and pull back, like a slingshot. The further you pull, the harder you hit. Let go to putt.

Keyboard: ← → to aim (hold Shift for fine aim), hold Space to charge, let go to putt. Esc to pause, Tab for the scorecard.

Hazards: 💧 water and 🕳️ space holes cost one extra stroke and put you back where you were. 🏖️ Sand slows you down, 🧊 ice is slippery.

Tricks: bounce off walls! Slopes push your ball, ⚡ arrow pads boost it, 🌀 portals teleport it and planets pull it in.

Score: try to get the ball in with as few strokes as possible. Under par is great. A hole-in-one is the best!

Max strokes: after 10 strokes the ball is picked up and you get 10 for that hole.
