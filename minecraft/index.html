Food Vending Machine

A Minecraft Java 1.21.1 food vending machine made with crafters. Open the files with the Litematica mod. Every version was tested in a real 1.21.1 server.

Machine + farms (newest)16 × 12 × 9. Chicken cooker, pumpkin, sugar cane and egg farms, honey and a fox berry pen fill the machine on their own. Machine + chicken farm15 × 12 × 5. Machine only15 × 5 × 3. You fill every chest yourself.

Buttons (left to right)

Sweet berries, cooked chicken, honey, cake, pumpkin pie, golden carrot, golden apple. Food comes out in the barrel at the bottom middle.

Setup after building (Litematica can't put items or animals in)

  1. Pie crafter: disable the bottom 6 slots and put 1 pumpkin, 1 sugar and 1 egg in the top 3.
  2. Gold crafters: put 1 gold nugget or gold ingot in each of the 8 outer slots, and carrots or apples in the middle. Fill both gold barrels with gold.
  3. Sugar crafter (behind the pie): disable every slot except the top-left one.
  4. Chicken farm (on top): put 2–4 chickens in the small glass box and fill the cauldron with a lava bucket. In the sorter hopper, put 18 cooked chicken plus 1 stick, 1 dirt, 1 sand and 1 cobblestone.
  5. Egg pen (behind the pie button): put 2 chickens in it.
  6. Plants: plant pumpkin seeds on the farmland and sugar cane on the mud next to the water.
  7. Honey: put bees in the beehive and plant flowers nearby. Fill the dispenser under the hive with glass bottles, leaving 1 slot empty. In the honey sorter hopper, put 4 honey bottles plus 1 stick, 1 dirt, 1 sand and 1 cobblestone.
  8. Berries: plant 2 sweet berry bushes on the mud in the left glass box and lead a fox in. This one is slow.
  9. Cake and gold: you stock these by hand. Machines can't milk cows, and gold needs a Nether farm.

Farms only grow while you are nearby, like every Minecraft farm.
