❔ HOW TO PLAY

Flippers: Z and / (or ← and →), or tap the left and right side of the screen. Launch: hold Space or ↓ (or the bottom right) and let go — the longer you hold, the harder it shoots.

Nudge the table with Shift (or swipe up) to save a ball — but nudge too much and you TILT!

Light the 3 top lanes for a bonus multiplier. Knock down all drop targets for big points. Sink the ball in the hole 3 times to start MULTIBALL!

Each table has its own missions shown at the top. You get 3 balls.
