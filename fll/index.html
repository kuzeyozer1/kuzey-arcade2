FIRST LEGO League Future Edition · Ortaokul / Lise

BIOGLOW · 2026–2027

This season is about biodiversity: how every gene, species and ecosystem is connected, and how to protect the web of life in nature. Teams spend 2 to 3 months on four parts, and each one is 25% of your tournament result.

🤖 Robot Game🛠 Engineering Design💡 Innovation Project🤝 Core Values

These are short explanations written for this app. The official files from Bilim Kahramanları Derneği always win if anything is different.

Who can join

  • 4 to 8 team members, aged 9 to 16, born between 1 January 2011 and 31 December 2017.
  • Only middle school and high school students. Primary school students can't join, even if their age fits.
  • A coach aged 18 or older, plus up to 2 advisers aged 17 or older (advisers are optional).
  • Middle and high school teams compete together. If even one member is in high school, the team counts as a high school team.
  • On tournament day only registered members, the coach and advisers can enter the area.
  • Registration is free. Only schools or institutions can register, from 1 October 2026, 11:00 at kayit.bilimkahramanlari.org.
  • Signed consent forms must reach the organisers 1 month before your tournament, or the team can't take part.

Tournaments

  • Local tournaments in Ankara, İstanbul and İzmir (two in each city), on weekends between 6 February and 21 March 2027. Your city decides which one you go to.
  • Tournament day: team registration 08:00–09:00, then robot matches from about 09:30 to 16:00.
  • Each team plays 3 robot matches of 2.5 minutes and has one 24-minute judging session.
  • Everyone gets a medal and a digital certificate.
  • The best teams go to the two-day National Tournament, and some winners there can go to international FLL events.

The four robot game roles

In the Future Edition robot game, your team plays four roles at the table. Each role has its own tool and missions.

Teknisyen

Mission 01 · Güçlü Mikrobiyomlar

Build a launcher with one motor and a colour sensor. Fire kilit türler into the Genç Orman mikrobiyomu.

Scoring: 20 points for each one inside (up to 3). The 4th one aims at the invasive queen on the other side: knock her over too and get a 40 point bonus.

💡 Aim beats speed: one careful shot is worth more than many quick ones. While practising you can put the forest and the hive on the same side; in a real match they face each other.

Uzman

Missions 02 and 03 · Büyük Ağaç

Use a remote to work the Büyük Ağaç, which has a double motor inside. Lift the kaynak jetonu up to the canopy, then turn the tree.

Scoring: 10 points for the token in the Kanopi Odacığı. Turning the tree so its branch opens the Mağara Şelalesi entrance lets kilit türler go through the waterfall.

💡 Small, careful moves. Line the tree up with your teammates so everyone can score more.

PDFRole sheet▶Video

Operatör

Mission 02 · Yenilenmenin Kökleri

Build a mechanical launcher with no motor: turn the crank back to store power, then flick it forward.

Scoring: 5 points for every kaynak jetonu that lands at the roots of the Büyük Ağaç.

💡 Change your aim and your power a little each time until the shots land the same way every time.

PDFRole sheet▶Video

Sürücü

Mission 05 · Merkez Sığınak

Drive a two-motor robot with a remote. Its front scoop collects kilit türler and kaynak jetonları from outside the Teknisyen area.

Scoring: 5 points for each piece that ends up completely inside the Merkez Sığınak.

💡 Smooth beats fast: drive gently so nothing falls out, then reverse back to the Teknisyen area for the next trip.

Project journey

Each role connects to real scientists who protect rainforests. Learn how they work, then use it in your design and your Innovation Project.

TeknisyenWildlife biologists: Motion cameras (fotokapan) and sound recorders let scientists watch endangered animals without disturbing them.PDFProject journey
UzmanForesters: GPS, mapping technology, sensors and an increment borer help them measure trees and see how forests change.PDFProject journey
OperatörPlant ecologists: Forceps and nets help them collect seeds without harming plants, which helps reforestation.PDFProject journey

Awards

Main awards

  • Şampiyonluk: the team that best shows the whole FLL experience
  • Mühendislik Tasarım: great robot design and coding
  • Öz Değerler: best Core Values
  • Robot Performans: highest robot match score
  • Proje: outstanding Innovation Project

Extra awards

  • Yükselen Yıldız: a team that grew a lot this season
  • Takım Çalışması: the two teams with the highest score together
  • İlham Verici Takım: a team that inspires others

Support awards

  • Aslan Koç: an outstanding coach or adviser
  • Duyarlı Profesyonellik / Akran: chosen by the other teams
