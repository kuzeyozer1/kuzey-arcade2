HOW TO PLAY

Move: arrow keys / WASD, or the stick on the left of the screen. You control the player with the ▼ arrow above his head — it switches to whoever is nearest the ball.

With the ball: X or 🔄 = pass to a team-mate. Space or ⚽ = shoot — hold it longer for a harder, higher shot. Aim with the stick while shooting.

Without the ball: X or 🔄 = slide tackle. C or 👤 = switch player.

Sprint: hold Shift or 💨. Sprinting uses stamina — watch the bar under your player.

Tips: the keeper is hard to beat straight on — shoot across the goal! Lob shots (hold shoot longer) can go over the keeper. Passing is faster than running.

Penalties: drag or use arrows to aim, then shoot. When you are the keeper, choose a side to dive!
