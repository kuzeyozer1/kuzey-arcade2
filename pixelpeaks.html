HOW TO PLAY

Move: ← → or A D (or the stick). Jump: ↑, W, Space or Z (or ⬆️). Hold jump to go higher.

Double jump: jump again in the air.

Wall jump (from World 2): slide down a wall and jump off it.

Dash (from World 3): Shift or X (or 💨) to zoom forward through the air.

Enemies: jump on slimes and bats. Spiky ones can't be stomped — avoid them! Turrets shoot at you.

Collect: 🪙 coins, 3 hidden 💎 gems per level, ❓ blocks (hit them from below) and 🚩 checkpoints. Reach the big flag to finish.

Hearts: you have 3. If you lose them all you go back to the last checkpoint.
