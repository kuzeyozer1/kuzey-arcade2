GOAL

PINE LAKE BANDIT

You are a raccoon living in the woods by Pine Lake. Sneak into the campground, raid picnic baskets, go fishing, sell your loot to the critters and buy cool gear. Explore Sunny Beach, Maple Farm, the Old Mine and secret Turtle Island, and the new Snowy Peak, Lighthouse Harbour, Carnival and Spooky Swamp! Cook in your den, help animal friends and watch the weather.

Move: drag on the left side of the screen (or WASD / arrows). Act: the big button (or Space). Humans see you inside their yellow light. Hide in bushes! Tap food in your bag to eat it.
