HOW TO PLAY

Goal: deliver the items on the top bar to the 📦 port.

Build: pick something in the bottom bar and tap the grid (drag to draw belts). ↻ or R rotates. 🗑️ or right-click removes (you get the money back).

⛏️ Miners go on ore patches and dig ore. Belts carry things in the direction of the arrows. Belts that point into another belt join it.

🔥 Smelters turn ore into plates (iron, copper), stone into bricks and sand into glass. Things go in from any side and come out of the front (the bright arrow).

⚙️ Assemblers make parts. Tap one to choose its recipe. They need all the right ingredients on belts going into them.

Splitters share one belt between left, right and forward. Crossings let two belts cross. Boxes store things.

Keys: 1–9 pick a building, R rotate, X remove, Space pause, F faster, arrow keys / drag with two fingers to move the camera, mouse wheel to zoom.
