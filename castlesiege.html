❔ HOW TO PLAY

Enemies march from the right along 5 lanes. Tap a troop card at the top, then tap a square to place it. Each troop needs 🪙 gold and a little time before you can place another.

⛏️ Miners make gold — place a few first! 🏹 Archers shoot down their lane, 🛡️ Knights block, 🧙 Wizards freeze, and there's more to unlock.

Tap your hero power (bottom left) for a big helping hand. Use the 🧹 broom card to remove a troop (you get half the gold back).

Every goblin that reaches the castle costs it a heart. Keep your castle standing until the last wave! P pauses, F speeds up.
