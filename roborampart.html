❔ HOW TO PLAY

Robots march from the red gates to your core. There is no road — your towers are the walls. Robots always take the shortest way around them, so build a long twisty maze and they'll walk past your towers for ages!

You can never block the way completely. If a tower would close the last path, the build spot turns red.

🧱 Walls are cheap and only block. Drones fly straight over everything — build towers that can hit the air (they show 🛩️).

Tap a tower to upgrade it (3 levels), choose who it aims at, or sell it. 📡 Boosters make the towers next to them stronger.

Call the next wave early with ▶ WAVE for bonus scrap. Your commander has three powers: ☄️ Orbital strike, 🧊 Freeze and 🔧 Repair.

Keys: 1–9 towers · Space next wave · Q W E powers · U upgrade · S sell · F speed · P pause.
