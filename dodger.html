❓ HOW TO PLAY

Steer by dragging anywhere (or the mouse, or ← → / A D). Dash with Space, the DASH button, or a double tap — you can’t be hit while dashing.

Near misses give a bonus coin. Zones change every 1000 km:

☄️ Asteroid Belt · 🧊 Ice Field (shards split in two) · 🌩️ Nebula Storm (lightning strikes the glowing lanes) · 💣 Mine Field (mines chase you) · 🔴 Laser Gates (fly through the gap) · 🕳️ Black Hole (it pulls you in). After that the loop starts again, faster!

Pickups: 🛡️ shield · 🧲 magnet · ⏳ slow time · ✖️2 double coins · 👻 ghost (fly through rocks) · 💣 bomb (clears the screen).

Every ship is different — try them all in the 🚀 Ships menu.
