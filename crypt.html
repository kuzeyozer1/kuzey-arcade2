HOW TO PLAY

Move: WASD (or the stick). Attack: arrow keys aim and attack in that direction — or click/tap where you want to attack. On a phone, ⚔ attacks the nearest monster.

Special: Space (or ✨) uses your hero's special power. It recharges over time. Bomb: E (or 💣) — bombs blow up rocks and hurt everything nearby.

Rooms: doors lock until every monster in the room is gone. 🟨 Treasure rooms have an item, 🛒 shops sell things for gold, 💀 the boss room leads down the stairs.

Keys: 🗝️ open locked treasure rooms and chests.

Items: every item makes you stronger. They stack — find combos!

Souls: even if you lose, you keep your 👻 souls to buy upgrades and new heroes.
