📖 BESTIARY

HOW TO PLAY

Goal: monsters walk along the road to your gate. Every monster that gets through costs lives (bosses cost more). Survive every wave to win.

Build: pick a tower in the panel, then tap a green spot next to the road. Tap a tower to upgrade it, change who it aims at, or sell it.

Upgrades: every tower has 3 levels, then you choose one of two special versions at level 4.

Damage types: arrows, cannons and soldiers do physical damage — armoured monsters 🛡 take less. Frost, fire, lightning and poison do magic damage — magic-resistant monsters ✨ take less. Mix your towers!

Flying monsters 🦇 can't be hit by cannons or soldiers. Ghosts 👻 are invisible until a Sniper, Mage tower or your hero spots them.

Hero: tap your hero, then tap the map to move. Heroes fight on their own and have two special powers (buttons in the panel).

Next wave early: press ⚔ NEXT WAVE before the timer ends for bonus gold.

Keys: 1–8 pick a tower, Space = next wave, F = speed, P = pause, Q/E = hero powers, Esc = cancel.
