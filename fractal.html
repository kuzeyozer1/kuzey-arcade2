WHAT IS THIS?

This is the Mandelbrot set. For every dot, the computer repeats one tiny maths rule, z → z² + c, hundreds of times. If the numbers stay small, the dot is coloured black. If they shoot off to infinity, the colour shows how fast that happened. That one simple rule makes shapes that never end: seahorses, spirals and tiny copies of the whole picture hidden inside itself. Shapes like this are called fractals, and you can find them in nature too, in ferns, snowflakes, lightning and coastlines.
