HOW TO FARM

Move: WASD / arrows or the stick, or just tap a square and your farmer walks there and uses the tool.

Tools (bottom bar, or keys 1–6): ⛏️ hoe the grass, 💧 water, 🌱 plant the chosen seeds, ✋ harvest / collect / pet, 🪓 chop trees and break rocks, 🎣 fish in the pond.

Crops: dig with the hoe, plant seeds, water every day. When it is ready, harvest with ✋. Rain waters everything for you! Each season has different crops.

Animals: build a coop or barn, buy animals, and fill their trough with 🌾 hay (make hay from wheat in the mill). Collect eggs, milk and wool with ✋.

Machines: put things in (✋ while holding the item) and come back later: mill, cheese press, jam pot, loom, oven.

House: use ✋ on your 🏠 to cook meals in the kitchen, upgrade your tools and see tomorrow's weather on the TV.

More fun: catch butterflies with the 🥅 net and donate them (and fish and crops) to the 🏛️ museum, give gifts to visiting villagers to become friends, follow ✉️ Grandpa's letters, and join a festival on the last day of every season.

Days: the day ends at midnight. Go to bed early with the 🏠 house (✋ on the door). Things in the 📦 shipping box are sold overnight.
