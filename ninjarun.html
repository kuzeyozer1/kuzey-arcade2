❔ HOW TO PLAY

Jump: tap the left side, Space, ↑ or W. Tap again in the air for a double jump.

Slide: swipe down, ↓ or S — slide under low bars!

Throw a star: tap the right side, X or Enter. Stars knock out enemy ninjas and birds. You can also jump on enemies.

Grab 🧲 magnets, 🛡️ shields, 💨 dash scrolls (run super fast and smash everything) and ✖️2 coins. The world changes as you run: bamboo forest, temple roofs, snowy peaks and the neon city!
