❔ HOW TO PLAY

Move left and right by dragging anywhere (or ← → / A D, or the mouse). You fire automatically.

Aliens fly in, line up, then dive at you. Shooting a diver scores double! 💥 Nova (Space or the NOVA button) clears the screen — you get one per stage.

Catch falling pods: 2 twin guns, W wide shot, L laser, 🛡️ shield, ⏳ slow time, ❤️ extra life.

Every 4th stage is a bonus stage: the aliens don't shoot, hit them all for a perfect bonus. Every 10th stage a mothership attacks!
