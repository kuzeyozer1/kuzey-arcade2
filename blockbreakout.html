BLOCK BREAKOUT

You're locked in Rock Bottom Prison. Mine your way to freedom: sell blocks, rank up from A to Z, and break out!

HOW TO PLAY

Mine. Tap or click a block near your prisoner (and hold) to break it. On a keyboard, walk into a block with ← → or A D to dig, press ↓ or S to dig down, and ↑ / W / Space to jump.

Sell. Blocks go in your backpack. When it's full, press SELL. Better blocks are worth more money.

Rank up. Spend money to go from rank A to Z. Every rank opens a deeper mine with rarer blocks: coal, iron, gold, diamonds, emeralds, and things even stranger.

Pickaxe. Upgrade Efficiency (faster), Fortune (more blocks per break), and Backpack (holds more). Tokens buy special powers like Explosive and Auto-Sell.

Crates. You sometimes find crate keys while mining, and you get a free key every day. Crates hold money, tokens and pickaxe skins.

Break out. Reach rank Z and BREAK OUT (prestige): you go back to rank A, but every block sells for more, forever.

Stuck in a hole? Press ⇧ TOP. When most of the mine is empty, it refills automatically.

What's inside
🪵 Start in Mine A, the wood mine (logs = 1 gold)
💰 Type /sell for gold, then grade up A to Z at the Grade Minister (Grade B = 60 gold)
🧑‍🌾 NPCs in the hub: Grade Minister, Tool Shop, Handyman (enchants), PvP Master, Armor Shop, Plot Archer
⚔ The PvP Mine: gold and diamonds, but players can beat you and take your blocks!
🏡 Buy a plot for 50,000 gold and build on it
⌨ Commands: /sell /mine /grade /gradeup /hub /pvp /plot /menu
👥 Friends who join get their own gold and grade

How to play it (iPad)
  1. Tap 📲 SEND THE WORLD TO MINECRAFT and pick Minecraft. It says "Import started", then "Successfully imported".
  2. Already had the old version? Delete the old Block Breakout Prison world first, then import this one.
  3. Open Minecraft → Play → Worlds → Block Breakout Prison.
  4. The first time, wait about 30 seconds while the prison builds itself around you (watch the % at the bottom).
  5. Mine logs, type /sell, then talk to the Grade Minister villager to reach grade B. Type /mine to go to your mine. The compass opens the menu.
  6. Friends: they join your world from the Friends tab. The add-on downloads to them automatically, and each friend starts at grade A.
If the world won't import, use the add-on instead: import the .mcaddon, then make a new world → World Type Flat → Behavior Packs → turn on Block Breakout → Create. The prison builds itself when you join.
