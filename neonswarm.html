❔ HOW TO PLAY

Keyboard + mouse: WASD or arrows to move, aim with the mouse and hold the button to shoot (or press T for auto-fire). Space or right-click dashes.

Touch: put a finger on the left half to move, and a finger on the right half to aim — your ship fires where you aim. Double-tap the right side to dash.

Shapes drop ✨ crystals. After each wave you spend them on upgrade cards: more bullets, more damage, orbiting blades, shields and more. Every 5th wave a boss appears!

Surviving waves earns 💠 cores for new ships and the Lab. P pauses.
