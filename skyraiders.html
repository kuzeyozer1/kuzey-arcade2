❔ HOW TO FLY

Move: drag anywhere on the screen (your plane follows your finger), the mouse, or arrows / WASD. Your guns fire by themselves.

💣 Bomb (Space or X, or the BOMB button): drops a bomb that falls onto tanks, trucks, guns and ships on the ground. Bullets can't hit ground targets well — bombs can!

🌀 Barrel roll (Shift or C, or the ROLL button): spin through bullets without getting hit. It needs a few seconds to recharge.

Power-ups: P makes your guns stronger, 💣 gives bombs, 🛡️ a shield, ❤️ repairs, 🛸 a wingman drone that flies with you, 🪙 coins.

Combo: shoot enemies quickly one after another for a score bonus. Trucks 🚚 carry extra coins!

P or Esc pauses.
