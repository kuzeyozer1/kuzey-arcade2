❔ HOW TO PLAY

Each turn you draw 5 cards and get 3 ⚡ energy. Play cards by tapping them (then tap an enemy if it's an attack). The number in the corner is the energy it costs.

⚔️ Attacks hurt enemies. 🛡️ Block stops damage until your next turn. ✨ Powers last the whole fight. Watch the icon above each enemy — it shows what they'll do next!

Statuses: 💪 strength (more damage), 🫥 weak (less damage), 💔 vulnerable (takes 50% more), ☠️ poison (hurts every turn), 🔥 burn.

Pick your path on the map, win cards and relics, and beat the boss of each act. If your hero falls, the quest ends — but you keep the 💎 gems.
