HOW TO DRIVE

Keyboard: ↑ or W = gas, ↓ or S = brake and reverse, ← → or A D = steer, Space = handbrake (start a drift), Shift or N = nitro, H = horn, P = pause, C = change camera.

Touch: use the stick on the left to steer. Gas is automatic (you can switch it off in the menu). Right side buttons: 🛑 brake, 💨 drift, 🔥 nitro.

Drifting: turn hard and tap the handbrake. Hold the slide — sparks go blue, then orange, then purple. Let go for a mini-turbo! Drifting also fills your nitro bar.

Track: grass and sand slow you down, ice is slippery, yellow arrow pads give a boost, ramps make you jump. Grab 🪙 coins on the road to buy cars.

Slipstream: drive right behind another car to get pulled along and fill nitro.
